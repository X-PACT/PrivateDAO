# Private Auctions

PrivateDAO Confidential Auctions let an organizer select a winner without exposing live bids while bidding is open.

## Create

Connect the organizer wallet, enter what is being bid for, choose a closing time, and create the auction.

## Invite and bid

The organizer authorizes bidder wallets. Each bidder connects the authorized wallet and submits a private amount. Other bidders cannot see the amount or ranking during the active session.

## Close and verify

After the deadline, the organizer closes the session. The final result is committed to Solana. Anyone can open the public receipt without a wallet.

Private bids are not displayed on the public receipt.
