# PrivateDAO Commercial Services Film - Human Voice Script

## Performance brief

- English, warm institutional male or female voice.
- Confident and human; never announcer-like or theatrical.
- Pace: approximately 145-155 words per minute.
- Leave short pauses after each commercial statement.
- Say `PrivateDAO` as one continuous brand name.
- Do not pronounce it as “Private D-A-O”.
- No claims beyond the wording below.

## Script

Your private work should stay private.

Salaries, decisions, bids, identity and financial records should not become
public simply because an organization uses modern infrastructure.

PrivateDAO helps organizations protect sensitive work while giving the right
people confidence in what happened.

Founded by Fahd Kotb, PrivateDAO is built around one standard:
private decisions, verifiable outcomes.

Pay your team, not the public.

Prepare payroll inside a protected workflow. Apply policy. Review. Approve.
Settle. Share the outcome without exposing individual salaries.

Make the decision privately.

Boards, foundations, teams and DAOs can protect participation before the
outcome is ready to reveal. Then prove the result when it matters.

Run sealed auctions.

Protect competitive intent until the right moment. Bids remain hidden, the
auction closes, and the verified result emerges without revealing everyone
else's private information.

Control the money. Protect the process.

Treasury requests move through budgets, policies, review, approval, execution
and a clear receipt. Private settlement keeps sensitive context bounded while
accountability remains visible.

Prove what matters. Reveal nothing else.

Blind Verification proves that a private condition was met. Record Verification
makes an important record checkable. The evidence can travel without exposing
the source.

Verify the person, not their entire life.

Private Identity Verification confirms eligibility, jurisdiction or authorized
representation while revealing only what the decision requires.

Agents need services too.

Through PrivateDAO Agent Exchange, software discovers a capability, requests
bounded work, receives a result and keeps a receipt it can verify.

Discover. Request. Execute. Verify.

Payroll, governance, auctions, treasury, settlement, verification, identity
and agent services become one operational experience.

Many services. One standard.

Underneath every workflow is the PrivateDAO Kernel. It separates what an
organization needs to do from the infrastructure that executes it.

Identity, policy, proof, evidence, signing and finality serve the work instead
of defining the user experience.

The workflow stays familiar while infrastructure can follow the job.

PrivateDAO is designed for organizations that need privacy, accountability and
room to operate across the ecosystems that fit their needs.

From one question to a working ecosystem.

Founded by Fahd Kotb and recognized by Superteam Poland and Superteam UAE,
PrivateDAO continues building with organizations, agents and the communities
shaping private infrastructure.

PrivateDAO.
