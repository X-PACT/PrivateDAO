01 · The problem

Payroll is a trust problem, not only a payment problem.

Organizations need to pay accurately and on time, but every payroll run also carries sensitive employee information, approval risk, reconciliation work, and audit pressure.

Too much exposure

Spreadsheets, exports and payment files can reveal more employee information than an auditor or board actually needs.

Too little assurance

Approvers need confidence that policy, deductions, budget and payout totals were handled correctly.

Manual proof

Finance teams repeatedly assemble evidence after the fact, slowing close and creating avoidable questions.

The market needs privacy without losing operational confidence.
