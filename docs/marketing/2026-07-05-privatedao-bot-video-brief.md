## Production Note

Most AI video tools generate short clips, usually between `4` and `10` seconds per prompt.

Best workflow:

1. Generate each scene separately.
2. Select the strongest take for each scene.
3. Stitch the clips in an editor such as `CapCut` or `Premiere`.
4. Add voiceover, sound design, transitions, and disclaimer.
5. Export a final `45-60 second` cut.
